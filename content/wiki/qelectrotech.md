Qelectrotech is a professional electrical scheme editor for Linux, Windows and macOS. Whether you are a planner, electrician or in DIY for electrical systems, Qelectrotech can provide you with clear documentation.

Unless to have access to proprietary tools like EPlan, Qelectrotech is your choice for documenting your electrical system. Some even extend the application for hydraulic system documentations. 

You can read about your questions and probably find your answers in the Qelectrotech wikipages. A [manual](https://download.tuxfamily.org/qet/manuals/online-manual_translation_pdf/) ist provided in many languages.  

Try it out, you will be surprised.

The development started in France, still the base team is french native, but the international community is growing fast. 
The potential of competing with all proprietary tools is given, already even for pure documentation, missing is some basic wiring calculation features (one can probably gain from the [KiCAD](https://www.kicad.org/) project).
