This page shows a list of architecture, engineering, construction & operations (AECO) workflow examples that involve the use of [FLOSS](/floss/) tools in their development process. The intention is to document successful cases of [FLOSS](/floss/) tools being used in (AECO) projects. These cases are from public sources like FreeCAD's forum and OSArch's forum. In some examples, they are just testing cases to study a tool performance. Other cases are real workflow in a production environment and have as result a built product. If you are adapting your workflow to FLOSS tools, you can study these cases to find useful tips, methods, ideas and files.

## 2D drafting
Many of the projects listed on this page started as 2D projects using [QCAD](/qcad/) or [LibreCAD](/librecad/). The [Getting started with 2D CAD drafting](/getting-started-with-2d-cad-drafting/) page explains more.

## Wikilab project by Uncreated Studio (2017)
The Wikilab project (2017) designed by [uncreated.net architecture network](http://www.uncreated.net/) and built. You can read [about the Wikilab](https://wikilab.blog.br/) and [about the Wikilab files (EN)](https://github.com/uncreatednet/wikilab-ufabc/blob/master/english.md) and then [download the Wikilab files](https://github.com/uncreatednet/wikilab-ufabc)/[translate site](https://translate.google.com/translate?hl=&sl=auto&tl=en&u=https%3A%2F%2Fgithub.com%2Funcreatednet%2Fwikilab-ufabc). You can even [View the model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/wikilab-01-bab56ed7d2414e13b3eacd6c2f29ce65) in 3d. The wikilab project uses the [wikihouse](https://www.wikihouse.cc/) construction principles. Feel free to take a look at the [PDF presentation of the wikilab](https://github.com/uncreatednet/wikilab-ufabc/blob/master/apresenta%C3%A7%C3%A3o/apresenta%C3%A7%C3%A3o%20online%20comunidade%20WikiHouse/wikilab.pdf) (in Portuguese).

## Open Source House by Studiolada (2020)
The [OpenSource House by Studiolada](/opensource-house-by-studiolada/) (2020) is a compact residential project by French architecture practice [studiolada](http://www.studiolada.fr/)

The project has been features on many architecture and design websites: [archdaily](https://www.archdaily.com/876104/open-source-house-studiolada-architects), [countryliving](https://www.countryliving.com/remodeling-renovation/news/g4636/open-source-house-free-plans/), [stylepark](https://www.stylepark.com/de/news/open-source-haus-von-studiolada)

A group of [FreeCAD](/freecad/) users have begun reconstructing the project as a way to drive knowledge and development in [FreeCAD](/freecad/). Feel free to join them and learn how it's done! https://forum.freecadweb.org/viewtopic.php?f=23&t=49030
