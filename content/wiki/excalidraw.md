Excalidraw is a whiteboard tool that lets you easily sketch diagrams that have a hand-drawn feel to them.

Our supporter alfredoangeles has started a [library of sketchy architectural objects](https://community.osarch.org/discussion/588/excalidraw-vgood-for-some-quick-sketch-like-architecture-tambien-en-espanol).

As this time (Maj 2021) Excalidraw does not support importing images. Keep an eye on the [Import images](https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/issues/19) issue to see how they're going with that.
