OpenStudio Measure is a scripting facility that uses Ruby and Python to automate model (.osm) queries and transformations, and to incrementally extend and customize the OpenStudio platform. Measures can be stored on the Building Component Library (BCL) (https://bcl.nrel.gov/), an open online database of OpenStudio related content like weather files, construction and equipment specifications.
