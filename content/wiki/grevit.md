"Build your BIM Model directly in [Grasshopper](/Grasshopper/). Grevit allows you to define BIM Elements in Grasshopper or [SketchUp](/SketchUp/) and translate them directly to Autodesk [Revit](/autodesk-revit/) or AutoCad Architecture. Grevit follows a one way process so your design model remains the geometrical source of truth: send geometry and attributes from [Rhino](/rhinoceros-3d/)/Grassopper or SketchUp to Autodesk Revit or ACA."
