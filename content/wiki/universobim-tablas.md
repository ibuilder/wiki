UniversoBIM Tablas is an addin for [Revit](/autodesk-revit/) that allows you to automatically create the model schedules and export them to Excel.
The addin consists of 3 tools:
- **Crear Tablas**: detects the Categories modeled in the project, and allows to create the Schedules automatically. You can choose between Quantity Schedule and Materials Take-Off. You can also select the parameters that appear in the Schedules.
- **Exportar Tablas**: shows a list with all the Schedules created in the project and allows them to be exported to Excel.
- **Sincronizar IDs**: synchronize a parameter called “id” of a family instance with its internal ID
