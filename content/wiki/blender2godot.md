## Blender2Godot
A free tool developed for quickly testing and deploying virtual / interactive visualizations, by linking Blender to the Godot Engine. Blender2Godot is a Blender3D addon that exports a Blender 3D scene to a Godot Engine project. This addon is in alpha state and thus still has a lot of bugs.
