## [OpeningDesign](https://hub.openingdesign.com/OpeningDesign/Bonsai_Tutorials#readme)
- [Ryan Schultz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ryanschultz/) is the founder of [OpeningDesign](http://openingdesign.com/), a radically transparent, open source architecture studio. He’s been practicing architecture for nearly 30 years, is a long-time contributor to the [OSArch community](https://osarch.org/), and has been [actively](https://community.osarch.org/discussion/1423/live-projects-an-exploration/p2) using Bonsai, in practice, for over 3 years.
