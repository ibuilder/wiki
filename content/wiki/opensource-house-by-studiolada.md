## Open Source House by Studiolada (2020)
The OpenSource House by Studiolada (2020) is a compact residential project by French architecture practice [studiolada](http://www.studiolada.fr/)

The project has been features on many architecture and design websites: [archdaily](https://www.archdaily.com/876104/open-source-house-studiolada-architects), [countryliving](https://www.countryliving.com/remodeling-renovation/news/g4636/open-source-house-free-plans/), [stylepark](https://www.stylepark.com/de/news/open-source-haus-von-studiolada)

## Join in to learn FreeCAD
A group of [FreeCAD](/freecad/) users have begun reconstructing the project as a way to drive knowledge and development in [FreeCAD](/freecad/). Feel free to join them and learn how it's done! The thread start here: https://forum.freecadweb.org/viewtopic.php?f=23&t=49030
