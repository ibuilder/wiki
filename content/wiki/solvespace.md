**SolveSpace** is an open-source parametric CAD tool for 2D drawing and 3D solid modeling.

- Website: https://solvespace.com
- Source: https://github.com/solvespace/solvespace
    - **Latest "nightly builds"**: https://git.io/SolveSpace3x (Linux, macOS & Windows)
- Reddit Community: https://www.reddit.com/r/SolveSpace
