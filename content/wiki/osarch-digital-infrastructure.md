## Live chat
We have both a Matrix room and an IRC channel (bridged together). The IRC channel is on [Libera.Chat](https://libera.chat/) and named #OSArch. The Matrix room is on [Matrix.org](https://matrix.org/)'s homeserver and is also named #OSArch. OSArch is a registered project under the Libera.Chat network, kindly registered by Libera staff member Christian (Fuchs).

Administration Matrix: [Duncan Lithgow](/User:Duncan/) ([dunlit on Matrix](https://matrix.to/#/@dunlit:matrix.org)), [Dion Moult](/User:Moult/) (moult on IRC), [Anton Tetov](/User:Tetov/) ([tetov on Matrix](https://matrix.to/#/@tetov:matrix.org) and tetov-irc on IRC)

Administration IRC: [Dion Moult](/User:Moult/) (moult on IRC), 

We can claim Libera channels using <code>/msg ChanServ CLAIM #channel</code>, and access the private Libera channel <code>#libera-communities</code> for other registered projects and communities.

For those on Matrix who want to use Libera-related benefits, such as claiming channels or IP cloaks, you would have to register a nickserv account on Libera and be identified to it, which is possible through Matrix [using this guide](https://matrix-org.github.io/matrix-appservice-irc/latest/usage.html#authentication).
