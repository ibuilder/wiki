Qex is a [Revit](/autodesk-revit/) plugin that obtains all the information from a BIM Model and creates a Quantity takeoff automatically.
## Characteristics
- Customize Quantity takeoffs
- Export to Excel
- Compatible with [Revit](/autodesk-revit/) 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020 and 2021
