## Architecture Classics modelled in FreeCAD
This list presents a group of 3D emblematic architecture models created with [FreeCAD](/freecad/) software, freely available from different sources and authors (forums, repositories, webpages, etc). The intention is to provide to users with open models that they can use and test in workflows with this parametric software. It is recommended FreeCAD 0.18 or higher to open these files.

See also [AECO Workflow Examples](/aeco-workflow-examples/)
