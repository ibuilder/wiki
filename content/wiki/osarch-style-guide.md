## Visual Identity
The final round of our community logo design process was closed on 2nd April 2021.

Fonts
- Headers are Open Sans (regular) [Open Sans](https://www.opensans.com/)
- Body Text is Lato Regular [Lato](https://www.latofonts.com/)

Curve color: 
- RGBA: e14658ff 
- RGB 225,70,88 
- CMYK C0 M69 Y61 K12
