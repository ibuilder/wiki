The Construction Operations Building Information Exchange (COBie) is a widely adopted US specification (an [IFC - Industry Foundation Classes](/ifc-industry-foundation-classes/) [MVD - Model View Definitions](/mvd-model-view-definitions/) for the exchange of IFC data to support facility management by owners and operators. A COBie file would typically be delivered as a spreadsheet.

There is some [COBie support](/bonsai-and-cobie/) in [Bonsai](/bonsai/). Also [IfcOpenShell](/ifcopenshell/) can make us of [COBie MicroMVD](/micromvds-for-exchange-requirements-cobie-micromvd/) and both can use [MicroMVDs](/micromvds-for-exchange-requirements/) for quality assurance of received data.

## Software Libraries
- [XbimCOBieExpress](https://github.com/xBimTeam/XbimCobieExpress) is a CPPL licensed library from [xBIMTeam](https://github.com/xBimTeam) to manage COBie data in a similar way that Xbim.Ifc does for IFC data
- [IfcOpenShell](/ifcopenshell/)
