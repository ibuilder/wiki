Salome-Meca is an open-source software environment combining [Salome](https://www.salome-platform.org/), a generic Pre- and Post-Processing platform for numerical simulation based on an open and flexible architecture made of reusable components, and [Code Aster](/code-aster/), an open-source finite element solver for structural and mechanical simulations.
