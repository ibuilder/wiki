## Textures & Models
These resources provide textures under a [Public Domain / Creative Commons Zero license](https://creativecommons.org/choose/zero/).
- [sharetextures.com](https://www.sharetextures.com/) has textures for architectural visualization.
- [3dtextures.me](https://3dtextures.me/) has textures for architectural visualization.
- [cc0textures.com](https://cc0textures.com/) has textures for architectural visualization.
- [3dassets.one](https://www.3dassets.one/) has textures for architectural visualization.
- [texturehaven.com](https://texturehaven.com/) has textures for architectural visualization.
- [3dmodelhaven.com](https://3dmodelhaven.com/) has useful 3D models.
- [cgees.com](https://cgees.com/) has 100% free HDRI skies for illuminating CG scenes.
- [hdrihaven.com](https://hdrihaven.com/) has 100% free HDRI skies for illuminating CG scenes.
- [cgbookcase.com](https://www.cgbookcase.com/) has Free PBR Textures.
- [texturecan.com](https://www.texturecan.com/) has Free PBR CG textures.
- [freestocktextures.com](https://freestocktextures.com/) has free textures.
- [resources.blogscopia.com](https://resources.blogscopia.com/) has free 3D models and textures.
- [Blender for Architecture](http://blender-archi.tuxfamily.org/Textures) textures for Blender.
- [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) Curated public asset library for visual effects artists and game designers.
- [architextures.org](https://architextures.org/) Web with textures.

## Procedural Textures
Software FLOSS to generate procedural textures.
- [Material Maker](https://rodzilla.itch.io/material-maker) Material Maker is a procedural materials authoring and 3D model painting tool, based on the Godot Engine. 
- [NeoTextureEdit](http://neotextureedit.sourceforge.net/) Easy to use graph-based procedural seamless texture editor.
- [TextureLab](https://njbrown.itch.io/texturelab) TextureLab is a tool designed to make procedural texture generation fast, simple and free.
- [BlenderInsight Procedural Materials](http://www.blenderinsight.com/). Free procedural textures for Blender! (*.blend files).
