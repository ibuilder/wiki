VI-Suite is a node-based add-on for performing environmental analysis in blender. The add-on acts as a pre/postprocessor for the [EnergyPlus](/energyplus/) and [Radiance](/radiance/) simulation engines. Stable Version (0.4) released for blender 2.7, a version for blender 2.8 is awaiting release.

VI-Sense was featured in the OSArch Monthly Meetup for November 2020. Recording: [Peertube](https://peertube.social/videos/watch/245c1e66-1980-4803-97bb-b1632e993f26) [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gdUQAGJZ8QM)
