ZCAD is a simple CAD program, written in Lazarus / FPC. It can open and save [Drawing Exchange Format (DXF)](/drawing-exchange-format-dxf/) (r2000) files. They are actively working on [Drawing (DWG)](/drawing-dwg/) support with the [LibreDWG](/libredwg/) library.

This project has no relation to the commercial ZWCAD CAD software.
