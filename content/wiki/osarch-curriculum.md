OSArch would love to be able to provide curriculum material for student and teachers to learn about the tools and processes of [OpenBIM](/openbim/) and [Free Software](/free-software/).

## Suggested Course Outlines
## Introduction to IFC Models
By the end of the course you should:
- Brief idea of what is IFC (see this video: [What is IFC? With Dion Moult](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kMpzrUJY7LU) on the [BIMvoice Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCPKI_VFw_UHYwEB3WeP69Sw))
- Know how to open IFC files (requirements: cross-platform, free software - so the only options are Bonsai / FreeCAD, I think)
- Know how to look at the element tree to see what's in a model
- Know how to navigate the 3D model (walking around, orbiting, zooming to fit)
- Know how to filter elements (hiding, isolating, highlighting elements)
