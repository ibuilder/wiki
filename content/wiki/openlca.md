"The world’s leading, high performance, open source Life Cycle Assessment software. openLCA is an open source and free software for Sustainability and Life Cycle Assessment, with calculation of your Sustainability Assessment and/or Life Cycle Assessment (LCA), detailed insights into calculation and analysis results, best in class import and export capabilities; easy to share your models
Life Cycle Costing and social assessment smoothly integrated in the life cycle model, and more."
