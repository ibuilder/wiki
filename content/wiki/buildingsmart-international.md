## Description
buildingSMART is a major standards organization for building information modelling. They are best known as the authors of [Industry Foundation Classes (IFC)](/ifc-industry-foundation-classes/), the standard for [openBIM](/openbim/) exchange of model geometry and data.

They are also the author of several other valuable standards for openBIM
- [IFC - Industry Foundation Classes](/ifc-industry-foundation-classes/)
- [BCF - BIM Collaboration Format](/bcf-bim-collaboration-format/)
- [bSDD - buildingSMART Data Dictionary](/bsdd-buildingsmart-data-dictionary/)
- [IDS - Information Delivery Specification](/ids-information-delivery-specification/)

There is an overview of [Methods to specify information requirements in digital construction projects](https://www.buildingsmart.org/methods-to-specify-information-requirements-in-digital-construction-projects/)
