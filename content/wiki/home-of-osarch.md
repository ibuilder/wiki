## Get Involved
{{< wiki-image src="/media/get-involved.svg" alt="class=homepage-icon" mode="frameless" align="center" >}}

Interested in OSArch initiatives, and why OSArch is so important to the design, construction, operation, and recycling of the built environment? See [an introduction to what OSArch is all about](/open-source-architecture-community/). We are a strong and growing community.

- Post questions in our [Community Forum](https://community.osarch.org/)
- Meet us at our online [Monthly Meetup](/monthly-meetup/)
- [Donate](/donation-directory/) to sustain some of the project we support
- See and add yourself to our list of [OSArch supporter](/osarch-supporters/)
- See [past projects](/aeco-workflow-examples/) using free software and see [Featured images](/featured-images/)
- See more ways to [contribute to OSArch](/how-to-contribute-to-osarch/)

Right now, we're looking in particular for volunteers to help with these things. If you're up for it, post on the forums and let us know!

- We need people to [help get the word out](/outreach/) and manage the OSArch accounts on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/OSArchOrg), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/osarch-org-121505200/), [Mastodon account](https://fosstodon.org/web/accounts/331798) & [Youtube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCoP6n8DdDajAM8YYDdFpQMQ).
- We need people to help [design a curriculum](/osarch-curriculum/) to teach about AEC tools and OpenBIM concepts.
- We need help with important unfinished wiki pages. Anybody can edit!
- And help with developement! Drop by the [community forum](https://community.osarch.org/) or [chat](https://osarch.org/chat/) to learn what projects are being talked about right now.


## Explore Free Software
{{< wiki-image src="/media/official-gnu.svg" alt="class=homepage-icon" mode="frameless" align="center" >}}

Did you know there's a growing list of over 100 free tools to help you do your job? Unlike proprietary software, [free software](/free-software/) will never make your digital work incompatible, obsolete, or force you into subscriptions. [See the AECO Free Software Directory](/aec-free-software-directory/).

## Use Open Standards
{{< wiki-image src="/media/open-standards.svg" alt="class=homepage-icon" mode="frameless" align="center" >}}

Open technology and standards helps our digital tools interoperate and protect you against data expiry. See what's available at the [Open Data Standards Directory](/aec-open-data-standards-directory/).

- Learn about [OpenBIM](/openbim/)
- Get sample files of [Open Data](/aec-open-data-directory/)
- Learn about relevant [Standards organizations](/standards-organizations/)
- Read [Academic Papers](/aec-academic-paper-directory/) and [external articles](/aec-articles/)

Learn about [IFC - Industry Foundation Classes](/ifc-industry-foundation-classes/) data concepts.

Delivering OpenBIM for facility management?

- [Learn about how COBie works](/construction-operations-building-information-exchange-cobie/)
- [Read the COBie definition of a maintainable asset](/cobie-definition-of-a-maintainable-asset/)
- [An introduction to Brickschema](/brick-schema/)

Learn how to specify data requirements with [MVDs](/mvd-model-view-definitions/) and [MicroMVDs](/micromvds-for-exchange-requirements/) ([all articles](/categories/model-view-definitions-mvd/)).

Stuck with proprietary software? Learn best practices and workarounds to produce industry standards compliant data. Read about how to [setup Revit for better OpenBIM](/revit-setup-for-openbim/)
